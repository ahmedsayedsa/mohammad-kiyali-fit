# AR Fit Blueprint

Design a luxury, high-converting personal brand and coaching landing page for "Anthony Rice" (AR Fit - Transformation Coach based in Fort Lauderdale). 

Target Audience:
Busy professional men and women who want to build lean muscle, strip stubborn body fat, and optimize their energy without extreme crash dieting or spending hours in the gym.

Visual Style & Aesthetic:
Modern dark-mode athletic luxury. Deep matte black (#0B0F17) background with sleek slate accents, clean white typography, and subtle athletic neon cyan or energetic amber highlights. Bold masculine/premium fitness typography, smooth micro-interactions, and high-end executive feel (resembling Equinox or Nike Training).

Structure & Sections:
1. HERO SECTION:
   - Strong Hook Headline: "Build Lean Muscle & Reclaim Your Peak Physique — Without Sacrificing Your Career or Lifestyle."
   - Sub-headline: "The bespoke 1-on-1 transformation system designed specifically for busy professionals. Science-backed nutrition, efficient hypertrophy training, and relentless accountability."
   - Primary CTA Button: "Start Your Transformation (Take Assessment)"
   - Secondary CTA: "Book a 1-on-1 Strategy Call"
   - Social Proof Badges: "39K+ Community | 500+ Busy Professionals Transformed | Verified Coach"

2. THE "AR FIT" 3-PILLAR METHODOLOGY:
   - Pillar 1: Precision Nutrition (Fuel your metabolism without bland meal plans)
   - Pillar 2: High-Yield Strength & Striking (Maximum muscle stimulation in minimum gym time)
   - Pillar 3: Executive Accountability & Lifestyle Integration (Daily check-ins, travel-proof habits)

3. TRANSFORMATIONS & PROOF:
   - Before/After visual comparison cards with client stats (e.g., "-24 lbs fat, +8 lbs muscle in 12 weeks").
   - Short quotes/testimonials from real busy entrepreneurs and executives.

4. TWO CLEAR CONVERSION PATHWAYS (Action Section):
   - Card A: "The AR Fit Questionnaire" — Quick 2-minute diagnostic to see if you qualify for the inner circle coaching program.
   - Card B: "1-on-1 Coaching Strategy Call" — Direct booking calendar embed to speak directly with Anthony.

5. FAQ SECTION:
   - Addressing common busy client objections: "What if I travel frequently?", "How many hours per week do I need?", "Do I need a gym membership?".

6. FOOTER:
   - Sleek minimal footer with copyright, links to Instagram (@anthonyricefit), TikTok, and a strong parting message: "Stop wishing. Start transforming."

## Project Overview
Official bespoke coaching landing page for AR Fit (Anthony Rice) built with modern React, TanStack, and Tailwind CSS.
